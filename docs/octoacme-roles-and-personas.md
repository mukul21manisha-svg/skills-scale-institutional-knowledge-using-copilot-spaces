# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **With Product Managers**: Clarify acceptance criteria and prioritization
- **With Project Managers**: Provide time estimates and track progress
- **With Quality Assurance Lead**: Collaborate on test strategy and quality standards
- **With Subject Matter Experts**: Seek technical guidance on domain-specific implementation

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **With Developers**: Clarify requirements and review implementation quality
- **With Project Managers**: Align on scope and timeline trade-offs
- **With Stakeholders/Product Owner**: Gather business requirements and feedback
- **With Communications Coordinator**: Communicate product strategy and updates

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **With Developers**: Track progress and identify blockers
- **With Product Managers**: Align on scope changes and prioritization
- **With Project Sponsor**: Report status and escalate organizational blockers
- **With Communications Coordinator**: Ensure consistent messaging to stakeholders
- **With Risk & Compliance Officer**: Monitor and mitigate project risks

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Project Sponsor/Executive Stakeholder

### Role Summary
Project Sponsors provide strategic direction and executive oversight for projects. They approve major decisions, secure organizational resources, and remove high-level blockers that prevent successful delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Approve project charter and major decisions
- Provide strategic direction and business context
- Secure budget and resource allocation
- Remove organizational blockers and escalations
- Review and validate project outcomes against business objectives

### Goals
- Ensure project aligns with organizational strategy
- Maximize return on investment
- Enable project team to succeed by removing constraints

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **With Project Manager**: Receive status updates and provide governance oversight
- **With Stakeholders/Product Owner**: Align on business requirements and success metrics
- **With Risk & Compliance Officer**: Monitor strategic risks and compliance requirements

### Project Phase Involvement
- **Initiation**: Approve project charter and business case
- **Planning**: Review and approve major plans and resource allocation
- **Execution**: Monitor progress and address escalations
- **Closing**: Validate outcomes and approve project closure

### Typical Communication
- Monthly executive status reviews
- Decision approval meetings
- Escalation and issue resolution

---

## Subject Matter Expert (SME)

### Role Summary
Subject Matter Experts provide specialized knowledge and guidance on technical, domain-specific, or organizational aspects. They ensure solutions are sound, compliant, and aligned with best practices.

### Responsibilities
- Provide technical or domain expertise on specialized topics
- Review designs and solutions for technical soundness
- Identify risks related to their area of expertise
- Mentor team members and share knowledge
- Ensure solutions align with best practices and standards

### Goals
- Ensure quality and soundness of specialized aspects
- Transfer knowledge to the team
- Reduce technical risks and rework

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **With Developers**: Review technical designs and provide guidance
- **With Quality Assurance Lead**: Advise on testing strategies for specialized areas
- **With Product Managers**: Validate feasibility and trade-offs
- **With Risk & Compliance Officer**: Identify compliance and risk implications

### Project Phase Involvement
- **Planning**: Advise on technical feasibility and approach
- **Execution**: Review work and provide ongoing guidance
- **Quality Assurance**: Validate solutions meet specialized standards

### Typical Communication
- Design review sessions
- Technical documentation and guidelines
- Ad-hoc consultations with development team

---

## Quality Assurance Lead

### Role Summary
Quality Assurance Leads define testing strategies, ensure quality standards are met, and validate that deliverables meet acceptance criteria and user expectations.

### Responsibilities
- Define testing strategy and quality standards
- Create and maintain test plans and test cases
- Coordinate testing activities across the team
- Identify defects and manage quality issues
- Report on quality metrics and test coverage

### Goals
- Ensure deliverables meet quality standards
- Reduce defects in production
- Maintain high test coverage and confidence

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **With Developers**: Collaborate on test strategy and acceptance criteria
- **With Product Managers**: Validate solutions meet requirements
- **With Project Managers**: Track testing progress and quality metrics
- **With Subject Matter Experts**: Understand specialized testing requirements

### Project Phase Involvement
- **Planning**: Develop test strategy and quality standards
- **Execution**: Execute tests and track quality
- **Release**: Validate readiness for deployment

### Typical Communication
- Test plan reviews and quality reports
- Defect tracking and resolution
- Quality metrics and trend analysis

---

## Stakeholder/Product Owner

### Role Summary
Stakeholders/Product Owners represent business needs and end-user perspectives. They prioritize requirements, provide feedback on deliverables, and ensure the solution addresses customer needs.

### Responsibilities
- Define business requirements and success criteria
- Prioritize features and requirements from business perspective
- Provide feedback and validation on deliverables
- Represent end-user and customer voice
- Approve acceptance criteria and sign-off on deliverables

### Goals
- Ensure solution delivers business value
- Maximize customer satisfaction
- Align project outcomes with business objectives

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **With Product Managers**: Collaborate on prioritization and requirements
- **With Project Managers**: Provide input on scope and approve changes
- **With Developers**: Clarify requirements and review implementation
- **With Communications Coordinator**: Stay informed on project progress

### Project Phase Involvement
- **Initiation**: Define business case and success metrics
- **Planning**: Prioritize requirements and define acceptance criteria
- **Execution**: Provide feedback and review progress
- **Closing**: Approve deliverables and validate business value

### Typical Communication
- Requirements review sessions
- Stakeholder briefings and updates
- Acceptance and sign-off meetings

---

## Communications Coordinator

### Role Summary
Communications Coordinators manage internal and external communications, maintain documentation, and facilitate knowledge sharing across the organization. They support project managers in keeping stakeholders informed and maintaining transparency.

### Responsibilities
- Develop and execute communication plans
- Create and maintain project documentation
- Coordinate status updates and stakeholder briefings
- Manage internal knowledge sharing and documentation
- Ensure consistent and clear messaging across teams

### Goals
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders
- Reduce miscommunication and confusion
- Preserve project knowledge for future reference

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **With Project Managers**: Support communication planning and execution
- **With Product Managers**: Help communicate product strategy and updates
- **With All Roles**: Gather information for status updates and documentation

### Project Phase Involvement
- **Initiation**: Develop communication plan and stakeholder list
- **Planning**: Communicate plans and expectations
- **Execution**: Maintain status updates and documentation
- **Closing**: Document lessons learned and project outcomes

### Typical Communication
- Status report preparation and distribution
- Project documentation and knowledge base management
- Stakeholder communication coordination

---

## Risk & Compliance Officer

### Role Summary
Risk & Compliance Officers identify, assess, and monitor risks and compliance requirements throughout the project lifecycle. They work with project managers and team leads to develop and implement risk mitigation strategies.

### Responsibilities
- Identify and assess project, technical, and organizational risks
- Monitor compliance requirements and regulations
- Develop and track risk mitigation strategies
- Escalate critical risks to project leadership
- Conduct risk reviews and maintain risk registers

### Goals
- Minimize project and organizational risk
- Ensure compliance with regulations and standards
- Enable informed decision-making on risk trade-offs

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **With Project Managers**: Identify, monitor, and mitigate project risks
- **With Project Sponsor**: Escalate strategic risks and compliance issues
- **With Developers**: Identify technical and security risks
- **With Quality Assurance Lead**: Ensure quality and risk standards are met

### Project Phase Involvement
- **Initiation**: Assess strategic and organizational risks
- **Planning**: Develop risk mitigation strategies
- **Execution**: Monitor and manage risk events
- **Closing**: Document residual risks and lessons learned

### Typical Communication
- Risk assessment reports and risk registers
- Risk review meetings and escalations
- Compliance status updates
